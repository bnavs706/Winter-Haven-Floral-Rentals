# Winter-Haven-Floral-Rentals
This is a website for the local business Haven Floral Rentals. It is designed to help customers book an appointment with the owner. Features planned to be included are payment handling and a calendar system of available days.
The current way to book appointments is strictly through DM. However this website will be the go to way of booking appointments and organizing events. Design wise it will be an elegant classy presentation matching the kinds of events the owner designs for. 


Upon entering the site the customer will be greeted with a “welcome” message to Haven Floral Rentals. A flower blooming animation will appear under the options the client will have to choose from.
Pieces that clients will be able to choose from will appear under a tab that states what kind of event the booking is for. They will have the option to choose from Wedding, Birthday, Bridal Shower, but they will also have the option to customize the request if the event doesn’t exactly match what is offered.


Clients are required to pay a down payment once the event is confirmed. The website will implement an automatic emailing system stating the event is confirmed. If the down payment is not received the customer will receive courtesy emails regarding the deadline to pay.
If the payment is still not received by deadline then booking is automatically cancelled. The vision is to lead customers from the Facebook and Instagram page to the website where all the events are scheduled, planned and processed.
Payments and storage of payment information could possibly be outsourced to squarespace.

CURRENTLY IN PROGRESS
Clients will be able to use the arch builder mode. They will be able to play with different options and create a rough mockup of what their arch will look like. You will be able to further modify specifically beyond the arch builder but it will help clients visualize the kind of arch they will be able to have. 

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION ABOUT EVENT PAGE
This section of the page will be organized as such, you will be able to see sample events and select from a series of presets to quickly plan and checkout. If sample events aren't to the customer's desire then that's were the arch builder mode will come in. 

Once the arch is fully customized there will be a review screen showing exactly what the customer ordered along with down payment. Once booked, they see a checkmark with all the information they need. Total cost, selected date and party package(the arch and whatever they added) displayed to them.

5 WEEK PLAN
The objective for the last weeks remaining will be to focus primarily on the packages for the arches.
(Pre-made packages)
The website will contain the premade packages clients can choose from. Such as Birthday, Wedding, Bridal Shower they will be able to see details on the package. The idea of this option is for a client who just wants to see already designed and available stuff to pick from rather than getting creative and designing
(Arch Builder)
This is for the particular clients who want to be more in depth with the design they choose from. Such as drapes, light fixtures perhaps they want easels they can design on to. The plan is for the client to add into their cart and edit the design to their liking, further development will include the other accessories clients can choose from
(Calendar)
This is will be designed around the business owner's schedule. They will be able to include their availability for user's to see and choose from.
(Data Base)
Allows client to see what is in use/ or currently available. Ideally a piece should always be available but if equipment gets damaged it can reflected on the site so there is no confusion on checkout
